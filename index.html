Open Minecraft, choose Multiplayer (Java) or Play → Servers (Bedrock), then add the server using the address below. Make sure your game version is supported by the server.

📜 Terms and Conditions

Please read carefully before placing an order on Echo SMP.

1. No returns, no refunds after delivery.
Once your items, ranks, or claim blocks are delivered in game, the sale is final. We do not accept returns and we do not issue refunds.
2. Cancel before acceptance.
You can cancel an order from My orders only until the sales team accepts and verifies your payment. After acceptance it cannot be cancelled.
3. Verification & Delivery.
Items are delivered after staff verify your payment screenshot in Discord. Your order automatically updates to Paid and Delivered in My orders.
4. Accurate Minecraft Username.
Always double-check your exact Minecraft Java or Bedrock username. Items delivered to an mistyped username cannot be transferred or refunded.
Questions? Join our Discord Support.
